Timeline

  1. ~4 Dec 2026FOI

    Reply due to our narrowed FOI

    On placement restrictions and the site assessment. See it.

  2. 6 Nov 2026FOI

    Narrowed FOI request submitted

    Two questions only, as the MoJ suggested: any placement restriction for people convicted of sexual offences against children, and the site assessment for the reopening. More narrowed requests will follow one at a time.

  3. 2 Nov 2026FOI

    MoJ refuses all three FOI requests on cost

    The MoJ treated our three requests as one and refused under section 12 (cost limit): We are unable to confirm if the MoJ holds the information you have requested within the cost limit. It said answering would mean examining numerous records across the Service relating to Ealing Approved Premises, including individual offender's records, going back to 2010. No exemption was applied, and it invited a narrower request: we may be able to answer a refined request within the cost limit. (MoJ ref 261012010)

  4. 13 Oct 2026HMPPS

    Hostel won't say if anyone lives there

    The AP Area Manager wouldn't share occupancy and referred our site questions to HMPPS and FOI.
