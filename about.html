Who we are

About us.

Local residents and parents from Corfton Road and Hanger Hill, not affiliated with any party. We support rehabilitation, but since autumn 2025 HMPPS has left most of our questions about this site unanswered.

01 / Principles

How we work.

  1. We support rehabilitation.
  2. We ask for transparency, site-specific safeguards and proper engagement.
  3. We use facts with sources, and we say clearly when something is unverified.
  4. We never identify, photograph, approach or speculate about any resident of the AP.
