02 / The facts

What we know.

Site
An Approved Premises (probation hostel) run by HM Prison and Probation Service (HMPPS). Source: gov.uk – London approved premises
Who lives there
Up to 22 men released from prison, assessed as high risk of serious harm. Sources: HMPPS CAS1 Approved Premises Policy Framework, Sept 2025, para 1.2; HMPPS letter to neighbours, 17 Aug 2026
Risk level
May house MAPPA Level 2 and 3 cases: those needing multi-agency risk management. Source: HMPPS letter to Dr Rupa Huq MP, 23 Jun 2026
Child sex offenders
HMPPS “cannot provide confirmation that child sex offenders will be excluded”. Source: HMPPS letter to Dr Rupa Huq MP, 23 Jul 2026
Schools
Nearest ≈320 m. 6 within 800 m; 12 within 1 km. See the map. Source: OpenStreetMap; ONS postcode data
Out and about
Residents can go out into the community. Standard curfew 11pm–6am. Sources: Policy Framework, Annex A, p.12; HMPPS letter, 3 Feb 2026
Open or not?
Due to reopen 24 Aug 2026. MP's office: postponed. Ward councillor: admitting since September. HMPPS won't confirm. Sources: HMPPS letters, 19 Nov 2025 and 17 Aug 2026; AP Area Manager, 13 Oct 2026
Consultation
No public consultation; HMPPS says none was required. Montpelier Primary says it wasn't consulted or informed. Sources: HMPPS letter to Dr Rupa Huq MP, 14 Apr 2026; Montpelier Primary, 7 Oct 2026
Policing
The Corfton Road area is now a ward police priority, with more foot patrols. Source: Police Ward Panel, Oct 2026 (reported by residents who attended)
What is an Approved Premises?

Temporary housing for people leaving prison who need close supervision. Over 100 in England and Wales. Rules include a curfew, 24-hour staff, CCTV, drug tests and room searches; breaking them can mean a return to prison.

Our concern isn't that they exist. It's whether this site, this close to schools, was properly assessed.

04 / What we want

  3. Set up a residents' liaison forum and a site visit.
  4. Explain how we'll be told about serious incidents.

Not against rehabilitation. We want to know if this site was properly assessed, and why we can't get answers.
