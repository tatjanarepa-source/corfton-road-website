01 / Summary

Summary

2 Corfton Road, Ealing W5, is an HMPPS Approved Premises for up to 22 men assessed as high risk of serious harm, near Ada Lovelace CE High School and other schools. Closed for about five years, it was due to reopen in August 2026; HMPPS won't confirm whether it is occupied. Residents have asked since autumn 2025 whether people convicted of sexual offences against children could be placed there. HMPPS says it "cannot provide confirmation" they will be excluded. Most questions remain unanswered; the MoJ refused our three FOI requests on cost grounds on 2 November 2026, and a narrowed request is due around 4 December 2026.
